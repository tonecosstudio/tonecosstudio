# UAT Quick Reference Card
## Production Management System

**Print this page and keep it handy during UAT sessions**

---

## Session Flow

### Admin Session (2-3 hours)
1. **Introduction** (15 min) - Welcome, consent, overview
2. **Scenarios** (90-120 min) - 7 guided scenarios
3. **Free Exploration** (15 min) - Let them explore
4. **Survey** (15 min) - Feedback form
5. **Wrap-up** (10 min) - Debrief and thank you

### Customer Session (30-45 min)
1. **Introduction** (10 min) - Welcome, consent, context
2. **Scenarios** (20-25 min) - 3 guided scenarios
3. **Survey** (10 min) - Feedback form
4. **Wrap-up** (5 min) - Debrief and thank you

---

## Key Phrases to Use

### Opening
✅ "We're testing the system, not you"  
✅ "There are no wrong answers"  
✅ "Your honest feedback is what we need"  
✅ "Feel free to think out loud"

### During Testing
✅ "What are you thinking right now?"  
✅ "What did you expect to happen?"  
✅ "How does this make you feel?"  
✅ "Take your time"

### When They're Stuck
✅ "What are you trying to do?"  
✅ "Where would you expect to find that?"  
✅ Wait 30 seconds before helping

### Encouraging Honesty
✅ "Please be completely honest"  
✅ "Negative feedback is just as valuable"  
✅ "You won't hurt anyone's feelings"

## What to Observe

### Behavioral Cues
- ✓ Facial expressions (confusion, frustration, delight)
- ✓ Hesitation before clicking
- ✓ Time spent on each task
- ✓ Where they look first
- ✓ What they try to click
- ✓ How they recover from errors

### Verbal Cues
- ✓ "I don't understand"
- ✓ "Where is...?"
- ✓ "This is confusing"
- ✓ "I like this!"
- ✓ "Why doesn't it...?"
- ✓ Unprompted comments

## Issue Severity Guide

### Critical 🔴
- System crash
- Data loss
- Cannot complete task
- Security issue
→ **Stop and document immediately**

### High 🟡
- Significant confusion
- Wrong result
- Major usability problem
- Requires workaround
→ **Note and continue**

### Medium/Low 🟢
- Minor confusion
- Cosmetic issue
- Unclear label
- Nice-to-have
→ **Note briefly**

## Success Indicators

### Good Signs ✅
- Completes tasks without help
- Positive verbal feedback
- Smiling, nodding
- Quick task completion
- Confident navigation
- "This is easy!"

### Warning Signs ⚠️
- Frequent confusion
- Asking for help repeatedly
- Frustrated expressions
- Long task times
- Getting lost
- "I don't understand"

---

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In
1. Check credentials
2. Check caps lock
3. Try password reset
4. Use backup account

### System Slow
1. Check internet
2. Clear cache
3. Try different browser
4. Note performance issue

### Participant Nervous
"Remember, we're testing the system, not you. There's no way to fail. Your honest reactions are exactly what we need."

### Participant Too Quiet
"I'd love to hear what you're thinking. Can you tell me what you're looking at right now?"

### Participant Rushing
"No need to hurry - we have plenty of time. Take your time to explore."

### Running Out of Time
- Prioritize critical scenarios
- Skip optional scenarios
- Extend if participant agrees

---

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do ✅
- Make participants comfortable
- Stay neutral
- Observe carefully
- Ask open-ended questions
- Document thoroughly
- Thank them frequently

### Don't ❌
- Interrupt unnecessarily
- Explain too much
- Defend the system
- Lead the participant
- Rush them
- Show disappointment
