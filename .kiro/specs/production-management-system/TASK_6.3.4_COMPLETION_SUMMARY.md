## Deliverables Created

### 1. UAT Execution Guide (`UAT_EXECUTION_GUIDE.md`)

**Purpose**: Comprehensive step-by-step guide for conducting UAT sessions

**Contents**:
- **Pre-Execution Checklist**: 2-week preparation timeline
  - Environment setup (staging, test data, accounts)
  - Documentation preparation
  - Participant recruitment
  - Session scheduling
  - Technical preparation
  - Team preparation
  - Final checks

- **Participant Recruitment**:
  - Admin user recruitment strategy and email templates
  - Customer user recruitment strategy and email templates
  - Confirmation email templates
  - Target profiles and selection criteria

- **Session Preparation**:
  - Room setup for in-person sessions
  - Remote session setup requirements
  - Materials checklist
  - Equipment requirements

- **Conducting Admin Sessions** (2-3 hours):
  - Detailed session structure
  - Introduction script (15 min)
  - 7 guided scenarios with instructions and observer notes:
    - A1: Dashboard Navigation (20 min)
    - A2: Character Choice Management (25 min)
    - A3: Production Stage Progression (30 min)
    - A4: Team Assignment (20 min)
    - A5: Blocking and Issue Management (20 min)
    - A6: Bulk Operations (15 min)
    - A7: Integration with OrdersPage (15 min)
  - Free exploration (15 min)
  - Feedback survey (15 min)
  - Wrap-up and debrief (10 min)

- **Conducting Customer Sessions** (30-45 minutes):
  - Streamlined session structure
  - Introduction script (10 min)
  - 3 guided scenarios:
    - C1: Character Selection (10 min)
    - C2: Order Progress Tracking (8 min)
    - C3: Mobile Experience (7 min)
  - Feedback survey (10 min)
  - Wrap-up (5 min)

- **Real-Time Issue Tracking**:
  - How to identify and document issues during sessions
  - Issue severity assessment guidelines
  - Quick issue capture form
  - When to stop vs. continue

- **Feedback Collection Process**:
  - Verbal feedback techniques
  - Behavioral observation tips
  - Survey administration
  - Follow-up interview guidelines

- **Post-Session Activities**:
  - Immediate actions (save recordings, create tickets)
  - Daily consolidation tasks
  - Weekly analysis activities
  - Final reporting requirements

- **Troubleshooting Common Issues**:
  - Technical issues (login, performance, features)
  - Participant issues (nervous, quiet, rushing, stuck)
  - Session management issues (time, emergencies)

- **Best Practices**:
  - Do's and Don'ts for facilitators
  - Success indicators to watch for
  - Warning signs to address

**Key Features**:
- Practical, actionable guidance
- Scripts and templates for consistency
- Timing guidelines for each activity
- Observer notes for what to watch
- Probing questions to ask
- Troubleshooting solutions
