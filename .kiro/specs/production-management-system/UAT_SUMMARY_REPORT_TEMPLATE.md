## UAT Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. Validate business requirements are met
2. Assess usability for admin and customer users
3. Identify UX issues and areas for improvement
4. Verify integration with existing systems
5. Collect user feedback for future enhancements

### Success Metrics
- 90% of test scenarios completed successfully
- Average usability rating ≥ 4/5
- Zero critical UX issues
- < 5 high-priority UX issues
- 80% of users complete tasks without assistance
